# Project Brief

## Project Title

E-commerce Growth Analytics & Purchase Propensity Modeling

## Business Background

Google Merchandise Store wants to use customer behavior data to improve its e-commerce conversion rate and optimize marketing resource allocation.

The business needs to understand where users drop off in the purchasing journey, which acquisition channels and customer segments generate the most value, and which sessions are most likely to result in a purchase.

## Business Question

How can an e-commerce company improve conversion rates by identifying funnel drop-offs, high-value customer segments, and high-purchase-intent sessions?

## Project Objectives

1. Analyze the customer purchase funnel.
2. Compare performance across acquisition channels and devices.
3. Evaluate customer behavior and product performance.
4. Identify meaningful customer segments.
5. Develop a model to predict purchase propensity.
6. Translate analytical findings into actionable growth recommendations.

## Data Source

This project uses the public Google Analytics 4 e-commerce dataset from the Google Merchandise Store.

The dataset contains event-level customer behavior data, including:

- Website and product views
- Add-to-cart events
- Checkout events
- Purchases
- Traffic sources
- Device information
- Geographic information
- Product and revenue data

## Key Deliverables

- Data quality assessment
- SQL data transformation and analysis
- E-commerce KPI framework
- Conversion funnel analysis
- Channel and device analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Purchase propensity model
- Interactive dashboard
- Executive summary
- Data-driven growth recommendations

## Project Scope

The project focuses on descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive analysis of customer behavior using the available GA4 sample data.

Because the dataset is anonymized and covers a limited time period, the project will not make causal claims or estimate long-term customer lifetime value without clearly stating the relevant limitations.
